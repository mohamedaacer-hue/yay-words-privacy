Yay Words: Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

In short

Yay Words collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no internet access, no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no sign-in. Everything stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Child profiles (name, birthday or age, and the color chosen for their pages), what each child has heard and played, the settings, and the recordings and photos you add are kept only on this device, in storage that only this app can read. They are never sent anywhere, and the app makes no backup copy. Anyone who can use this device can open the app and see them, so keep the device locked when you are not with it.

The microphone and photos

The app uses the microphone only when a grown-up taps Record, to record a word, a name or a song. Photos are used only when a grown-up adds one in Our family. Recordings and photos are kept only on this device.

Keeping and removing

Everything stays until you remove it. Deleting a child’s profile removes its progress and name recording. Family photos, recordings and songs can be removed on their own pages. Removing the app removes everything. We never receive a copy, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete.

Children

Yay Words is made for young children playing with a grown-up nearby. It shows no ads, sells nothing and has no links out of the app. The settings are for grown-ups.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new policy comes with an app update and shows the date it changed.
